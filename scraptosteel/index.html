FAQ

Where is my game saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app or starting a “New Game” also deletes your save, diamonds and purchased items.

My purchased diamonds did not arrive. Email us the purchase date and your App Store receipt. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off ad tracking? Turn off Scrap to Steel’s permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
